        Review

        Every faction's turn is a decision an AI agent made. Several metrics score it automatically (DeepEval), and you can add your own judgment below each one — that's what "annotation" means here.

        Insights

        A cross-game view: how does each role preset tend to score, averaged over every decision any faction with that preset has made in any evaluated game so far? This is what actually answers "does the Warmonger really play less legally than the Diplomat-Trader" — not just in one playthrough.
